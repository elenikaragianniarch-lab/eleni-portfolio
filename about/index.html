I build working systems: archives, agents and pipelines that turn data into images, films and spaces. Most of my work asks how places are lived, seen and remembered, and by whom. Before the Bartlett I drew real buildings in Athens and Thessaloniki, and worked on an AI safety startup.

I’m looking for a role in London in applied AI, computational design or creative technology. I have full UK right to work.
