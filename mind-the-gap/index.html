Mind the Gap

We mapped the gaps between buildings across Thessaloniki and found residents already using them, with improvised staircases. The project turns that habit into a scaffolding system that attaches to any gap and carries a small public programme.

Year
2023
With
Katerina Aggelaki, Paschalina Iliadou
Type
Studio project, University of Thessaly
Site
Thessaloniki, Greece

What residents do

Vacant lots and leftover spaces showed the same informal pattern again and again: staircases that residents had added to make use of space nobody else used. Our job was to notice their solution and make it repeatable.

The library

The library module is developed in plans, elevation and section.
