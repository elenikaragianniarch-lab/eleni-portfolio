Night Walk

Four people with different needs walk the same streets after dark. Each one finds their own best route, then they negotiate one route that works for all of them.

A design prototype built on open data. It is not safety advice.

Method

Every street is scored from 0 to 1 on five criteria from OpenStreetMap and police.uk data. Each walker turns those scores into a cost per metre using their own weights, and the route is found with Dijkstra's algorithm. To agree, each walker's own best route is a candidate, plus routes built from their shared priorities. The chosen route is the one where the walker who gives up the most gives up the least (minimax regret). Steps rule a route out for anyone who needs step-free access.
