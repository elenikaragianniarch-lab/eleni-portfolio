        What the data can't see

        Method

        Every street is scored from 0 to 1 on five criteria from OpenStreetMap and police.uk data. Each walker turns those scores into a cost per metre using their own weights, and routes are found with Dijkstra's algorithm. The "everyone" route is chosen by minimax regret: the candidate where the walker who gives up the most gives up the least. Steps rule a route out for anyone who needs step-free access. The city map comes from 2,560 simulated trips (160 random starting points, 16 destinations each, 600 m to 2.2 km), each routed both ways.
